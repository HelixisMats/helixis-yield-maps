How the estimate is made. Annual heat = DNI × aperture area × optical efficiency × (1 − loop losses), the same model as the Helixis thermal calculator. Apertures: LC10 HW 10.2 m², LC20 HW 20.4 m². Dual-axis tracking means the collector sees the full DNI.
Data. Long-term average DNI from Global Solar Atlas (Solargis model, data version 2.2.68, updated April 2026), sampled on a 0.05° grid (about 5.5 km) and interpolated between points. Values vary strongly over short distances in the volcanic north-west and the Nyungwe highlands; check the exact site before quoting. Shading over Lake Kivu is irradiance only.
Scope. A first-look estimate for screening sites, not a performance guarantee. Real output also depends on operating temperature, heat demand, shading, wind parking and availability. For an offer, run a site study on hourly data.
